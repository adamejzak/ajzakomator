# unimux 0.9.4

- **Every cell of a big grid draws on the GPU.** Grids with more than 12 terminals used to draw the extra cells through the much slower DOM renderer. Now all cells of the largest 5x4 grid get a WebGL renderer: with 20 busy shells the window's main thread spends about 15-25% less time per second, layout work drops from about 35 ms per second to zero and the slowest frame drops from about 20 ms to 12 ms. If the GPU ever allows fewer contexts, the focused cell and the cells with fresh output get them first, a cell keeps a new context for a few seconds (no flickering back and forth), and a terminal whose GPU context was lost comes back on WebGL after a short pause.
- **Error cards follow the language.** If the status bar or a dialog fails to draw, its "try again" card now switches language together with the rest of the app.
